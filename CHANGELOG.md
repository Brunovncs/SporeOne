# Changelog

## 0.0.4

The game's folder and the Launcher Kit's can be chosen by hand in the settings, for when SporeOne
does not find them, and changed when there are several copies. Buttons and fields have softer
corners and more room around their text.

## 0.0.3

Finds a Launcher Kit that was just installed, before its launcher has been opened once, and the
GOG copy of the SPORE Collection.

## 0.0.2

A cleaner window. The catalog is a list by default, with mods installed or updated from their row,
and a grid one click away. Mods not in the catalog can be added from a GitHub link or a file, and
suggested for the catalog with a GitHub issue; approved suggestions are indexed on their own.
SporeOne now tells when a new version is out and updates itself. "Play with mods" is now "Play".

## 0.0.1

The first version. A catalog of 140 Spore mods built from the GitHub repositories tagged
`spore-mod` and a list kept by hand, each installed in one click into the Launcher Kit, with the
mods it needs. Updates, uninstalling, options for mods that have them, mods dropped on the window,
and SporeCoop through its own installer. Says before the download whether a mod fits the game, the
store it came from and the Launcher Kit. In English and Portuguese.
