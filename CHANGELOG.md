# Changelog

## 0.0.1

The first version. A catalog of 140 Spore mods built from the GitHub repositories tagged
`spore-mod` and a list kept by hand, each installed in one click into the Launcher Kit, with the
mods it needs. Updates, uninstalling, options for mods that have them, mods dropped on the window,
and SporeCoop through its own installer. Says before the download whether a mod fits the game, the
store it came from and the Launcher Kit. In English and Portuguese.
